# Gen Ads

## Asked for

- Owner: a **Gen Ads** button on every landing page in /admin/pages that makes the AI analyse the page and produce the ads concept: post captions, a master idea for the poster design, and more ideas, to make ad creation much faster.

## Done

- Three independent designs (a marketer's, an art director's, an engineer's) were judged and merged before building; the result is [[Gen Ads]].
- **Landing Pages CMS → Gen Ads** on each card → `/admin/pages/<id>/ads`: one AI call writes the package (analysis, three concepts with poster text and captions, video script, Google and LinkedIn text, objection posts, first Telegram reply, targeting, A/B tests); the code computes every number (offer lines, photo prompts, Canva pack, carousel cards, Google fact headlines, posting plan) from the CMS at render time.
- Number check on every AI line: poster and ad slots with a number not on the page are emptied and counted; long texts are kept and flagged; owner-typed extra facts are allowed. Flags also for stray placeholders, a price in the sales reply, superlatives and platform limits.
- Stored per page with one previous version (restore); first open runs the generation by itself, later runs are an explicit click, 6 per ten minutes.
- **Create tracked links**: one campaign per channel per page with three ad versions (`utm_content = concept-a/b/c`), reused, never duplicated.
- The Ad Poster Kit opens on a page from `?page=<slug>`; CopyButton and PromptBox became shared components with a clipboard fallback for in-app browsers.
- Texts in English and Khmer (`gen.*`).

## Verified

- Typecheck, lint, 331 unit tests (brief extraction in both languages and for classic pages, normalizing the AI answer, dropped and flagged slots, typed live numbers, closed-offer concepts, live offer lines, carousel cards, posting plan in Phnom Penh time, storage, restore, the one-run lock, campaign creation that survives long slugs, underscores and renames).
- A five-angle review (correctness, security, honesty, texts, phone use) with adversarial verification: 37 agents, 3 findings confirmed, all fixed, among them a crash on the first open without a package, campaign slugs that did not survive the store's `slugify`, video lines escaping the number check, a duplicate paid run on reload, and an Anthropic retry that could outlive the route's 300 seconds.
- Local build and browser check with a seeded package (32 steps): the card button, every tab, flags on invented numbers, live CMS offer lines, tracked links created once, Google fact headline, the first open without a package, the passed-deadline page, the no-key path (message, link, 503 `no_key`), admin-only API, sticky tab row on laptop and phone, the page card footer on a phone, phone width without horizontal scroll.
- Not run against the real AI from here (no key in the test setup): the owner's first click on a live page is the first real run.
