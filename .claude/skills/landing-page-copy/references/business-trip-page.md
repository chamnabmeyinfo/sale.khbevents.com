# Copy formulas for a business-trip / delegation page

Every formula shows the pattern, then an example written for the Vietnam
Smart City, Tea and Cafe trip. Swap the facts, keep the shape.

## Hero
Headline: [Outcome] in [time] at [place]. Under 12 words.
> Come home with suppliers, not just photos.

Sub-headline: who it is for + the three things they do there.
> A 4-day B2B trip for Cambodian café, tea and retail-tech owners: two
> international expos, factory visits and matched supplier meetings in Hanoi.

Primary CTA: verb + object + risk reversal.
> Reserve my seat, no payment today

Risk line under the CTA: three short facts separated by dots.
> No payment today · Seat held instantly · Coordinator replies in 15 min

## Proof strip
[count] business owners already in · [roles in the room]
Count comes from `urgency.claimedSeats`. Never type the number.

## Core value cards (3 or 4)
Title: noun phrase naming the outcome. Body: how, with a number, then the
consequence for their business in one sentence.
> Factory-direct pricing. Buy at the source and cut 25 to 35% off what brokers
> charge. One container order can pay for the whole trip.

## Problem section
Tag: the cost of the status quo. Title: a claim with a number. Three problems
in the buyer's language, then a one-line bridge to the solution.
> Why importers who source online pay up to 35% more.

## Audience cards
Title: the reader's job title as they would say it. Body: what they source
there. End with one line that says who should not come, it raises trust.

## Value stack
Title: "One price. [N] things handled." List each inclusion with the price it
would cost alone. Total, then the seat price. The gap is the argument.

## Itinerary
Day title = business first, leisure second.
> Cafe Show Expo and supplier meetings, then Hanoi Old Quarter

## Seat roster
Title names the real capacity. Legend words: Booked / Open / Yours.

## Testimonials
Quote with a number or a named result, then name, role, company. If you do not
have real ones, show the roster and the organiser's track record instead and
tell the user the section is hidden until real quotes exist.

## Pricing
One anchor (standalone total or regular price), one live price, one line on what
is not included. Plan names say when they apply, not marketing words.

## Three steps
1. Reserve (60 seconds, no payment). 2. Call (who, when, what you receive).
3. Fly (what to bring, what is handled).

## Guarantee
Three bullets: pay only after the call, invoice and itinerary sent, full refund
if the organiser cancels. Plus the cancellation rule for the buyer, honestly.

## Register form
Headline: the action. Sub: who calls and how fast. Two required fields. A pill
with seats left. The coordinator's name. A Telegram button as the alternative.
Success message: thank them by name if you have it, say who calls and when.

## FAQ order
Visa · Do I pay today · Invoice · Room · What if seats sell out · What is not
included · Refund · Language · Can I bring a partner or staff · Passport validity.

## Final CTA
[seats left] seats left. [claimed] business owners are already in.
Then the consequence of waiting, then the same CTA as the hero.
